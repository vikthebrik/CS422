# MCC Calendar Hub — 5-Minute Live Demo Script

**Format:** Cue sheet. Each presenter reads **Talking Points** aloud while performing **Actions**. Transitions are scripted — read them exactly to hand off cleanly.

---

## SECTION 1 — Regular User (Public Student)
**Presenter:** [Name] | **Time:** ~1 minute | **Logged in as:** None (public view)

---

**Action:** Open the app to the main calendar grid. Events are visible immediately, no login required.

> **Talking Point:** "This is what any student on campus sees the moment they land on the site — no account, no friction. Every club event, all in one place."

---

**Action:** Click the filter sidebar. Select two clubs. Watch the calendar update in real time.

> **Talking Point:** "Students can filter down to exactly the clubs they care about. If you follow three orgs, you're not sifting through noise."

---

**Action:** Click a single event to open the detail modal.

> **Talking Point:** "Clicking any event surfaces everything you need — description, time, location. And if the event requires a ticket or RSVP..."

**Action:** Point to the RSVP / ticket link in the modal.

> **Talking Point:** "...there's a direct link. One tap. Done."

---

**[TRANSITION — read aloud]:** *"That's the student experience. Simple, fast, no login required. Now let's go behind the curtain and see how a club officer actually puts those events on the calendar."*

**[Hand off to Admin presenter. Admin logs in.]**

---

## SECTION 2 — Admin User (Club Officer)
**Presenter:** [Name] | **Time:** ~2.5 minutes | **Logged in as:** Club Officer account

---

**Action:** Land on the club dashboard. The officer's club name and logo are visible at the top.

> **Talking Point:** "Once a club officer logs in, they see their organization's home base. Their events, their settings, their presence on the platform."

---

**Action:** Click "Edit Club" and update the Instagram or Linktree link. Save.

> **Talking Point:** "Social links, bio, contact info — all editable here. Changes appear publicly on the club's page instantly."

---

**Action:** Click "New Event." Fill in a title, date, location, and toggle RSVP on. Paste in a fake ticketing link. Save.

> **Talking Point:** "Creating an event takes about 30 seconds. And that RSVP toggle is what we just saw from the student side — the officer sets it here, the student clicks it there."

---

**Action:** Navigate to the ICS Sync section of the club settings. Show the Outlook/Google Calendar feed URL field.

> **Talking Point:** "Here's the part that saves clubs the most time. Instead of manually posting every event, an officer can paste in their Outlook or Google Calendar feed URL — and the platform syncs automatically every 14 minutes. New event on their calendar, it appears here. Edit or cancel it there, it updates here."

> **Talking Point:** "No double-entry. No stale listings."

---

**Action:** Navigate to the Collaborations tab. Show a pending collab request from another club. Accept it.

> **Talking Point:** "Clubs don't always host events alone. The collaboration system lets one club send a co-host request to another. When accepted, the event appears on both clubs' pages and in both clubs' calendar filters."

**Action:** Point to the accepted event now showing both club logos/names.

> **Talking Point:** "The audience sees two clubs, one event. That's a real workflow we built around how MCC actually operates."

---

**[TRANSITION — read aloud]:** *"Club officers manage their own lane. But someone has to manage the officers — approve new clubs, keep the platform healthy. That's the root admin. Over to you."*

**[Hand off to Root presenter. Root logs in.]**

---

## SECTION 3 — Root User (System Administrator)
**Presenter:** [Name] | **Time:** ~1.5 minutes | **Logged in as:** Root admin account

---

**Action:** Open the Club Management panel. Show the list of pending account requests with club name, contact email, and message.

> **Talking Point:** "When a new club wants to join the platform, they submit a request from the public site. The root admin sees it here — club name, contact, their message. One click to approve."

**Action:** Approve a request. A confirmation appears.

> **Talking Point:** "On approval, the system automatically creates their account and emails them their credentials. No manual password setup, no back-and-forth."

---

**Action:** Navigate to the About page. Show the live CMS with content blocks — text, media, and club showcase sections.

> **Talking Point:** "The public About page is fully editable by the root admin through this block-based CMS. Add a text block, drop in a photo, reorder sections — no code, no deploy."

**Action:** Make a small live edit to a text block. Save. Open the public About page in another tab to show the change live.

> **Talking Point:** "That edit is live right now to every visitor on the site."

---

**Action:** Navigate to the server status dashboard briefly (show sync history / cache state).

> **Talking Point:** "And for the technical side — there's a live status dashboard showing the last sync run, cache state, and log history. When something looks off, this is the first place we look."

---

**[CLOSING — read aloud, single presenter or together]:**

> *"Three roles, one platform. A student finds and RSVPs to events in seconds. An officer keeps their calendar in sync without double-entry. An admin keeps the whole system healthy without touching code. That's MCC Calendar Hub."*

## Presenter Notes

- **Rehearse transitions aloud** — the handoff lines are where live demos usually stall.
- **Pre-log in all three accounts** in separate browser tabs before starting. Tab switching is faster than logging in on stage.
- **Don't narrate clicks.** Say the talking point *while* performing the action, not after.
- **If something breaks:** The Regular User section requires no login and no write operations — it's your safe fallback to keep talking while recovering.
