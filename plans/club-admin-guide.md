# MCC Calendar Hub — Club Admin How-To Guide

A complete checklist for club officers from first setup through day-to-day use.

---

## Part 1 — Setting Up Your Outlook Calendar (ICS Feed)

Your events are pulled automatically from a shared Outlook calendar. This only needs to be done once.

- [ ] Open **Outlook** (web or desktop) and sign in with your UO account
- [ ] In the left sidebar, click **"Add calendar"** → **"Create blank calendar"**
- [ ] Name it something identifiable (e.g. "SSWANA Events – Public")
- [ ] Right-click the new calendar → **"Sharing and permissions"**
- [ ] Set sharing to **"Can view all details"** and copy the **ICS link** that appears
  - The link ends in `.ics` and looks like: `https://outlook.office365.com/owa/calendar/...`
- [ ] Save this link — you'll paste it into the MCC site during setup

> **Note:** Only events added to this specific shared calendar will sync to the MCC site. Your personal calendar is not affected.

---

## Part 2 — Getting Your Account

- [ ] Go to **[uomcc.org](https://www.uomcc.org)** → click **"Admin Sign In"** → **"Request an Account"**
- [ ] Fill in your club name and contact email, and optionally a short message
- [ ] Submit the form — the MCC admin team will review your request
- [ ] Once approved, you will receive an email from **MCC Calendar Hub** with a **"Reset Password"** link
- [ ] Click the link → set your password (min. 8 characters) → you're in

---

## Part 3 — Logging In

- [ ] Go to **[uomcc.org](https://www.uomcc.org)** → click **"Admin Sign In"** (top right)
- [ ] Enter your email and password → click **Sign In**
- [ ] You will be taken to the **Club Management** page automatically
- [ ] Your club name and a key icon will appear in the top navigation bar

**Forgot your password?**
- [ ] Click **"Admin Sign In"** → **"Forgot Password?"**
- [ ] Enter your email → click **"Send Reset Link"**
- [ ] Check your inbox for an email from MCC Calendar Hub → click **"Reset Password"**
- [ ] Set a new password

---

## Part 4 — Connecting Your Outlook Calendar

This links your club's events to the MCC site so they sync automatically.

- [ ] Click your club name in the top navigation bar (or go to **Clubs → your club**)
- [ ] Click the **pencil (Edit) icon** near the top of your club page
- [ ] Paste your Outlook **ICS URL** into the **"Outlook ICS URL"** field
- [ ] Click **Save**
- [ ] Within ~14 minutes, your events will appear on the site (syncs every 14 minutes automatically)

> **Tip:** If your events don't appear after 15 minutes, double-check that the ICS URL is from a *publicly shared* calendar, not a private one.

---

## Part 5 — Setting Up Your Club Profile

- [ ] Navigate to your club page (click your club name in the nav bar)
- [ ] Click the **pencil (Edit) icon**
- [ ] Fill in or update:
  - [ ] **Description** — a short paragraph about your organization
  - [ ] **Instagram** — your Instagram handle or full URL
  - [ ] **Linktree** — your Linktree URL (or any general link-in-bio URL)
  - [ ] **Engage** — your UO Engage page URL
- [ ] Click **Save**

**Uploading a Logo:**
- [ ] In the same edit modal, look for the **Logo** section
- [ ] Click **"Upload Logo"** → select an image file (PNG or JPG recommended)
- [ ] The logo will upload and appear on your club page and event cards

---

## Part 6 — Managing Events

### Events synced from Outlook (automatic)
Events you add to your shared Outlook calendar will appear on the site within ~14 minutes. No manual action needed.

### Manually editing a synced event
Once an event is on the site, you can refine its details:

- [ ] Find the event on the calendar or on your club page → click it to open the event page
- [ ] Click **"Edit Event"**
- [ ] Update any of:
  - [ ] **Title**
  - [ ] **Description**
  - [ ] **Location**
  - [ ] **Event Type** (General, Cultural, Workshop, Social, etc.)
  - [ ] **RSVP Required** toggle — turn on if attendees need to register
    - [ ] **RSVP / Ticket Link** — paste the registration URL (Google Form, Eventbrite, etc.)
    - [ ] **RSVP Note** — optional message shown to attendees (e.g. "RSVP by Friday noon")
- [ ] Click **Save Changes**

> **Note:** Manually edited fields (title, description, location, event type) will NOT be overwritten by future calendar syncs. Only the start/end times are refreshed from Outlook.

### Creating an event manually (without Outlook)
- [ ] Go to your club page → click **"Add Event"** (top right of the events section)
- [ ] Fill in all fields including date, time, location, and event type
- [ ] Optionally toggle RSVP on and add a link and note
- [ ] Click **Create Event**

### Deleting an event
- [ ] Open the event page → click **"Edit Event"** → scroll to the bottom → **"Delete Event"**
- [ ] Confirm deletion

> **Warning:** If the event is synced from Outlook, it will reappear on the next sync unless you also remove it from your Outlook calendar.

## Part 7 — Collaborations

When another club lists your organization as a collaborator on their event, you'll receive a collaboration request.

**Reviewing incoming requests:**
- [ ] Click **"Collab"** in the top navigation bar
- [ ] You'll see a list of pending collaboration requests from other clubs
- [ ] For each request, click **"Accept"** or **"Reject"**
- [ ] Accepted events will show your club's logo as a co-host

**Adding a collaborating club to your own event:**
- [ ] Open the event page → click **"Edit Event"**
- [ ] Scroll to the **"Collaborating Clubs"** section
- [ ] Select a club from the dropdown → click the **+** button
- [ ] The other club will receive a collaboration request; once they accept, their logo appears on the event

---

## Part 8 — Building Your Team Roster

The "Our Team" section on your club page lets you showcase your members publicly.

**Adding a member:**
- [ ] Go to your club page → scroll to the **"Our Team"** section
- [ ] Click **"Add Member"**
- [ ] Fill in:
  - [ ] **Name**
  - [ ] **Title** (e.g. "President", "Community Outreach Chair")
  - [ ] **Section**: Executive Team / Board / Interns
  - [ ] **Email** (optional — visitors can click to copy, not visible as raw text)
- [ ] Click **Save**

**Uploading a member photo:**
- [ ] Click the **pencil icon** on an existing member card
- [ ] Click **"Upload Photo"** → select an image
- [ ] Photos are automatically resized and optimized (max 300px)

**Reordering members:**
- Members appear in the order they were added within each section
- To change order, edit a member and adjust their position by deleting and re-adding in the desired sequence

**Removing a member:**
- [ ] Click the **pencil icon** on the member card → click **"Delete"** → confirm

---

## Part 9 — Account Settings

**Changing your password:**
- [ ] Click the **key icon** in the top navigation bar
- [ ] Enter your current password, then your new password twice
- [ ] Click **Save**

**Changing your email:**
- [ ] Click the **key icon** → scroll to the **"Change Email"** section
- [ ] Enter your new email → click **Update**
- [ ] A password reset email will be sent to your new address to confirm the change

---

## Part 10 — Day-to-Day Workflow

Once set up, your routine as a club admin is minimal:

- [ ] **Add events to your Outlook calendar** as you normally would — they sync automatically
- [ ] **Edit event details** on the site if you need to add an RSVP link, update a description, or fix a location
- [ ] **Check the Collab tab** when hosting joint events — accept collaboration requests from partner clubs
- [ ] **Keep your club profile updated** — update your description, logo, or social links each term
- [ ] **Maintain your team roster** — add/remove members at the start of each academic year

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Events not showing up | ICS URL not set, or calendar not shared publicly | Re-check Part 4; ensure the calendar is shared with "Can view all details" |
| Events disappeared | Club's ICS URL changed in Outlook | Update the ICS URL in your club edit modal |
| Can't log in | Wrong email or password | Use "Forgot Password?" to reset |
| Edit button not visible | Not logged in, or viewing a different club's page | Sign in with your club admin account |
| Collaboration not showing | Other club hasn't accepted yet | Ask the other club to check their Collab tab |
| RSVP link not appearing on event | RSVP toggle is on but link field is empty | Edit the event and paste the registration URL |
